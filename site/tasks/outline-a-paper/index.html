Questions about outline a paper with AI

How do HWS students use AI for outlining a paper?

21 use cases across 19 of the 42 majors at Hobart and William Smith Colleges come down to this one task — 8 easy, 12 medium, 1 hard by difficulty. Getting the shape of an argument down before writing a word of it. Every entry below links to the use case on its major's page, where it comes with a tutorial and a starter prompt written for that field.

Which AI tool is best for outlining a paper?

Any of the free general-purpose assistants — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — handles this. The starter prompts here are written to work in all three, so pick whichever you already have an account for rather than shopping for a specialist tool.

Is it allowed to use AI for outlining a paper at HWS?

That is your professor's call and it varies by course and by assignment. Check the syllabus first. These are study aids meant to help you learn faster, not a route to work you submit as your own — when the syllabus does not say, ask before you use it.
