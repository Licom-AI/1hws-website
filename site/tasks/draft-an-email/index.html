Questions about draft an email with AI

How do HWS students use AI for drafting an email?

40 use cases across 40 of the 42 majors at Hobart and William Smith Colleges come down to this one task — 40 easy by difficulty. Writing the awkward email you have been putting off for three days. Every entry below links to the use case on its major's page, where it comes with a tutorial and a starter prompt written for that field.

Which AI tool is best for drafting an email?

Any of the free general-purpose assistants — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — handles this. The starter prompts here are written to work in all three, so pick whichever you already have an account for rather than shopping for a specialist tool.

Is it allowed to use AI for drafting an email at HWS?

That is your professor's call and it varies by course and by assignment. Check the syllabus first. These are study aids meant to help you learn faster, not a route to work you submit as your own — when the syllabus does not say, ask before you use it.
