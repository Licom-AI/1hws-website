Career Services

Virtual Joint Interview Essentials and Networking 101 Workshops

Monday, August 31, 2026 · 6 PM–7:30 PMVirtualSalisbury Center for Career, Professional and Experiential Education

Event details

This combined workshop, hosted by Career Services, satisfies both prerequisites, Interview Essentials and Networking 101, for students applying for Guaranteed Internship Funding. Students will learn how to build and maintain a professional network, approach networking opportunities with confidence, and effectively use platforms such as LinkedIn to expand professional connections. The workshop will also cover interview essentials, including different types of interviews, how to prepare, professional attire, responding to behavioral and general interview questions, and best practices for following up with employers after an interview. Whether you are actively applying for internships or planning ahead, this workshop will equip you with practical tools to make strong professional connections and present yourself effectively to employers—while fulfilling a key requirement for Internship Funding.
