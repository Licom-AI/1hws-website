The linked tutorial covers MacMost's copy-paste method — pull an article into reader view, paste it into ChatGPT after 'summarize this:', then ask for a set word count or a 10-bullet version; also how to grab a YouTube transcript to summarise a video.

Define theological terms

Clarify concepts like soteriology, theodicy, and eschatology. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Religious Studies student at HWS. My task: Define theological terms — Clarify concepts like soteriology, theodicy, and eschatology.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.

Religious Studies AI questions, answered

How can Religious Studies students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Religious Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as summarize a quranic surah and define theological terms through to project-scale work like construct an original comparative framework. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Religious Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Religious Studies that means summarize a quranic surah, define theological terms and explain the four noble truths. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes compare creation narratives and build a comparative religion study guide.

What do I need to start using AI as a Religious Studies student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, and nothing else — no coding, no paid subscription, no special software. Every use case on this page links to one of 13 tutorial videos that walks through the technique, and ships with a starter prompt already written for Religious Studies coursework.

Is it OK to use AI for Religious Studies coursework at HWS?

It depends on the course, and the professor decides. Policies differ between instructors and even between assignments, so check the syllabus before using any of these on graded work. The use cases here are study aids — a prompt like "Construct an original comparative framework" is meant to help you learn the material faster, not to produce work you hand in as your own. When in doubt, ask first.
