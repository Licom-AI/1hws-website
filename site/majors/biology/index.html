Biology AI questions, answered

How can Biology students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Biology students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain natural selection to a peer and summarize a reading on cellular respiration through to project-scale work like analyze dna sequence data with bioinformatics tools. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Biology major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Biology that means explain natural selection to a peer, summarize a reading on cellular respiration and define ecology vocabulary. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes build a study guide for a genetics exam and compare pcr and gel electrophoresis methods.

What do I need to start using AI as a Biology student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Biology use cases here draw on 12 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Biology coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Biology coursework at HWS?

Your Biology professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Analyze DNA sequence data with bioinformatics tools" exists to get you through Biology material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
