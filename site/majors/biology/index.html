Easy

Explain natural selection to a peer

Clarify the mechanism with a concrete example. The linked tutorial covers a 10-step ChatGPT prompt system — standard definition, an explain-it-to-a-10-year-old version, real-life analogies, a simple diagram, then a comparison table — that you aim at any concept you're stuck on.

What you'll take awayA repeatable prompt sequence that turns a confusing idea into a definition, an analogy and a diagram you can check against your course notes.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Biology student at HWS. My task: Explain natural selection to a peer — Clarify the mechanism with a concrete example.

Start with a one-sentence definition, then explain it as if I'm 10, then give a real-world analogy. Finish by flagging anything scholars genuinely disagree about — and tell me what you're unsure of.

Biology AI questions, answered

How can Biology students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Biology students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain natural selection to a peer and summarize a reading on cellular respiration through to project-scale work like analyze dna sequence data with bioinformatics tools. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Biology major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Biology that means explain natural selection to a peer, summarize a reading on cellular respiration and define ecology vocabulary. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes build a study guide for a genetics exam and compare pcr and gel electrophoresis methods.

What do I need to start using AI as a Biology student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, and nothing else — no coding, no paid subscription, no special software. Every use case on this page links to one of 12 tutorial videos that walks through the technique, and ships with a starter prompt already written for Biology coursework.

Is it OK to use AI for Biology coursework at HWS?

It depends on the course, and the professor decides. Policies differ between instructors and even between assignments, so check the syllabus before using any of these on graded work. The use cases here are study aids — a prompt like "Analyze DNA sequence data with bioinformatics tools" is meant to help you learn the material faster, not to produce work you hand in as your own. When in doubt, ask first.
