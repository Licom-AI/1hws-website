Easy

Define ecological terms

Explain trophic cascade, eutrophication, or carrying capacity with examples. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer, and the starter prompt below already frames it for Environmental Science.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you — enough to use on Environmental Science work this week.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Environmental Science student at HWS. My task: Define ecological terms — Explain trophic cascade, eutrophication, or carrying capacity with examples.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.

Environmental Science AI questions, answered

How can Environmental Science students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Environmental Science students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as define ecological terms and summarize a scientific paper through to project-scale work like run gis spatial analysis code. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Environmental Science major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Environmental Science that means define ecological terms, summarize a scientific paper and explain the carbon cycle. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes analyze a water-quality dataset and compare remediation methods.

What do I need to start using AI as a Environmental Science student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Environmental Science use cases here draw on 13 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Environmental Science coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Environmental Science coursework at HWS?

Your Environmental Science professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Run GIS spatial analysis code" exists to get you through Environmental Science material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
