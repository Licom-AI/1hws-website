Music AI questions, answered

How can Music students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Music students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain a music theory concept and summarize a composer biography through to project-scale work like model acoustic properties. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Music major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Music that means explain a music theory concept, summarize a composer biography and define genre terms. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes analyze a score harmonically and compare performance interpretations.

What do I need to start using AI as a Music student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Music use cases here draw on 14 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Music coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Music coursework at HWS?

Your Music professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Model acoustic properties" exists to get you through Music material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
