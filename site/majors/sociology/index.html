Define sociological concepts

Clarify anomie, habitus, social capital, and structural strain. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer, and the starter prompt below already frames it for Sociology.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you — enough to use on Sociology work this week.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Sociology student at HWS. My task: Define sociological concepts — Clarify anomie, habitus, social capital, and structural strain.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.

Sociology AI questions, answered

How can Sociology students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Sociology students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as define sociological concepts and summarize a durkheim reading through to project-scale work like build a custom qualitative-coding gpt. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Sociology major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Sociology that means define sociological concepts, summarize a durkheim reading and explain the difference between sex and gender. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes analyze a census dataset and compare sociological theories.

What do I need to start using AI as a Sociology student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Sociology use cases here draw on 12 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Sociology coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Sociology coursework at HWS?

Your Sociology professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Build a custom qualitative-coding GPT" exists to get you through Sociology material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
