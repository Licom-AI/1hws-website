20 practical, difficulty-rated ways Gender and Feminist Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges can use AI — each naming the exact tutorial it links to and what you’ll take away. Click any card to watch it.

Before you use these on graded work: check your professor’s policy on AI. It differs by course, and these examples are study aids — not permission.

All Gender and Feminist Studies AI use cases

Easy

Define key theoretical terms

Explain intersectionality, the gender binary, or performativity clearly. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Gender and Feminist Studies student at HWS. My task: Define key theoretical terms — Explain intersectionality, the gender binary, or performativity clearly.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.
