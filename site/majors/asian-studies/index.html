Asian Studies AI questions, answered

How can Asian Studies students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Asian Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as summarize a reading on confucian ethics and explain the concept of guanxi through to project-scale work like model historical trade-network relationships. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Asian Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Asian Studies that means summarize a reading on confucian ethics, explain the concept of guanxi and translate a short passage for comprehension. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes compare political systems across east asian states and build a study guide for a religions-of-asia exam.

What do I need to start using AI as a Asian Studies student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Asian Studies use cases here draw on 14 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Asian Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Asian Studies coursework at HWS?

Your Asian Studies professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Model historical trade-network relationships" exists to get you through Asian Studies material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
