AI Use Cases for Psychological Science at HWS

Learn more about the Psychological Science program ↗

20 practical, difficulty-rated ways Psychological Science students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges can use AI — each naming the exact tutorial it links to and what you’ll take away. Click any card to watch it.

Before you use these on graded work: check your professor’s policy on AI. It differs by course, and these examples are study aids — not permission.

Easy

Explain a psychological concept

Clarify cognitive dissonance with relatable examples. The linked tutorial covers a 10-step ChatGPT prompt system — standard definition, an explain-it-to-a-10-year-old version, real-life analogies, a simple diagram, then a comparison table — that you aim at any concept you're stuck on.

What you'll take awayA repeatable prompt sequence that turns a confusing idea into a definition, an analogy and a diagram you can check against your course notes.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Psychological Science student at HWS. My task: Explain a psychological concept — Clarify cognitive dissonance with relatable examples.

Start with a one-sentence definition, then explain it as if I'm 10, then give a real-world analogy. Finish by flagging anything scholars genuinely disagree about — and tell me what you're unsure of.

Summarize a journal article's methods and findings

Note the sample, the design, and the limits the authors acknowledge. The linked tutorial covers MacMost's copy-paste method — pull an article into reader view, paste it into ChatGPT after 'summarize this:', then ask for a set word count or a 10-bullet version; also how to grab a YouTube transcript to summarise a video.

What you'll take awayHow to compress a long reading or video into a summary of the exact length you need, and why pasting the source keeps it accurate.
