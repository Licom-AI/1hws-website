Psychological Science AI questions, answered

How can Psychological Science students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Psychological Science students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain a psychological concept and summarize a journal article's methods and findings through to project-scale work like build a custom research-methods gpt. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Psychological Science major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Psychological Science that means explain a psychological concept, summarize a journal article's methods and findings and define operant conditioning, schema, and heuristic. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes design a survey instrument and compare theoretical perspectives.

What do I need to start using AI as a Psychological Science student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Psychological Science use cases here draw on 12 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Psychological Science coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Psychological Science coursework at HWS?

Your Psychological Science professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Build a custom research-methods GPT" exists to get you through Psychological Science material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
