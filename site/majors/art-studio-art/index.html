AI Use Cases for Art: Studio Art at HWS

Learn more about the Art: Studio Art program ↗

20 practical, difficulty-rated ways Art: Studio Art students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges can use AI — each naming the exact tutorial it links to and what you’ll take away. Click any card to watch it.

Before you use these on graded work: check your professor’s policy on AI. It differs by course, and these examples are study aids — not permission.

Easy

Brainstorm concepts for a mixed-media series

Generate thematic directions and material ideas for a new body of work. The linked tutorial covers three brainstorming methods in ChatGPT — a reusable custom-instructions 'brainstorm paradigm', an AI-diagram tree that fans one topic into dozens of branches, and a SWOT prompt to pressure-test an idea.

What you'll take awayThree prompt patterns you can reuse to go from a blank page to a categorised list of options, plus a way to stress-test the one you pick.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Art: Studio Art student at HWS. My task: Brainstorm concepts for a mixed-media series — Generate thematic directions and material ideas for a new body of work.

Give me 10 options grouped into 3 themes, then mark the 3 most original and explain what makes them stronger.

Easy

Write an artist statement draft

Turn scattered notes into a coherent one-page statement. The linked tutorial covers AI Academy's approach to long-form writing: never ask for the whole essay at once — break it into smaller prompts across two tabs and a document so you keep control of the argument.

What you'll take awayA workflow that automates the writing but not the thinking, so the argument stays yours and the draft stays checkable.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Art: Studio Art student at HWS. My task: Write an artist statement draft — Turn scattered notes into a coherent one-page statement.

Help me write this, don't write it for me. First ask me 3 questions about my argument, then propose a thesis and a section plan. Do not draft full paragraphs until I approve the plan.

Explain color theory concepts

Draft a gallery submission email

Compose a professional inquiry to a local gallery or juror. The linked tutorial covers drafting an email in ChatGPT and improving it through conversation — the first draft is deliberately generic, then detail is added turn by turn — plus what not to paste in for privacy.

What you'll take awayWhy the first draft always reads like AI, how to iterate it into your own voice, and to keep real names out of the prompt.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Art: Studio Art student at HWS. My task: Draft a gallery submission email — Compose a professional inquiry to a local gallery or juror.
