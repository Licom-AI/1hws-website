How can American Studies students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for American Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a American Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For American Studies that means summarize a chapter on american exceptionalism, explain the concept of manifest destiny and brainstorm topics linking pop culture and politics. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes compare consensus and conflict schools of history and synthesize sources on consumer culture in the 1950s.

What do I need to start using AI as a American Studies student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 American Studies use cases here draw on 17 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for American Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for American Studies coursework at HWS?

Your American Studies professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Build a custom GPT on American cultural theory" exists to get you through American Studies material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own.
