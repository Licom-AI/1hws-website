20 practical, difficulty-rated ways Media & Society students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges can use AI — each naming the exact tutorial it links to and what you’ll take away. Click any card to watch it.

Before you use these on graded work: check your professor’s policy on AI. It differs by course, and these examples are study aids — not permission.

Easy

Summarize a McLuhan reading

Condense a dense media theory chapter into plain takeaways. The linked tutorial covers MacMost's copy-paste method — pull an article into reader view, paste it into ChatGPT after 'summarize this:', then ask for a set word count or a 10-bullet version; also how to grab a YouTube transcript to summarise a video.

What you'll take awayHow to compress a long reading or video into a summary of the exact length you need, and why pasting the source keeps it accurate.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Media & Society student at HWS. My task: Summarize a McLuhan reading — Condense a dense media theory chapter into plain takeaways.

Give me (1) a three-sentence summary, (2) five bullet-point key claims, and (3) anything the author asserts without supporting evidence. Text below:

Define hegemony, framing, and agenda-setting

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you.

I'm a Media & Society student at HWS. My task: Define hegemony, framing, and agenda-setting — Definitions anchored to a recent news example for each.

Draft a pitch email

Write an outreach email to a podcast producer for internship. The linked tutorial covers drafting an email in ChatGPT and improving it through conversation — the first draft is deliberately generic, then detail is added turn by turn — plus what not to paste in for privacy.

What you'll take awayWhy the first draft always reads like AI, how to iterate it into your own voice, and to keep real names out of the prompt.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Media & Society student at HWS. My task: Draft a pitch email — Write an outreach email to a podcast producer for internship.
