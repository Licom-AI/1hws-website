Individual Major AI questions, answered

How can Individual Major students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Individual Major students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain your interdisciplinary framework and brainstorm a cross-disciplinary topic through to project-scale work like model an integrated theoretical system. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Individual Major major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Individual Major that means explain your interdisciplinary framework, brainstorm a cross-disciplinary topic and summarize a reading from another field. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes synthesize sources across fields and build a personalized study plan.

What do I need to start using AI as a Individual Major student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Individual Major use cases here draw on 16 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Individual Major coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Individual Major coursework at HWS?

Your Individual Major professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Model an integrated theoretical system" exists to get you through Individual Major material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
