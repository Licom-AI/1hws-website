Anthropology AI questions, answered

How can Anthropology students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Anthropology students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as summarize an ethnography chapter and explain the concept of cultural relativism through to project-scale work like build a custom gpt trained on ethnographic methods. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Anthropology major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Anthropology that means summarize an ethnography chapter, explain the concept of cultural relativism and brainstorm topics for a kinship research paper. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes compare functionalism and structuralism and synthesize articles on medical anthropology.

What do I need to start using AI as a Anthropology student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Anthropology use cases here draw on 15 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Anthropology coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Anthropology coursework at HWS?

Your Anthropology professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Build a custom GPT trained on ethnographic methods" exists to get you through Anthropology material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
