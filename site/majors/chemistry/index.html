Chemistry AI questions, answered

How can Chemistry students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Chemistry students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain reaction mechanisms and define chemistry terms through to project-scale work like simulate reaction equilibria. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Chemistry major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Chemistry that means explain reaction mechanisms, define chemistry terms and summarize a journal article. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes interpret nmr spectra and build a thermodynamics exam study guide.

What do I need to start using AI as a Chemistry student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Chemistry use cases here draw on 15 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Chemistry coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Chemistry coursework at HWS?

Your Chemistry professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Simulate reaction equilibria" exists to get you through Chemistry material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
