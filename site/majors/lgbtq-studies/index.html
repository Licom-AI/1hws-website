20 practical, difficulty-rated ways LGBTQ+ Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges can use AI — each naming the exact tutorial it links to and what you’ll take away. Click any card to watch it.

Before you use these on graded work: check your professor’s policy on AI. It differs by course, and these examples are study aids — not permission.

Easy

Define cisnormativity, heteronormativity, and intersectionality

Plain definitions with how each shows up in everyday institutions. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a LGBTQ+ Studies student at HWS. My task: Define cisnormativity, heteronormativity, and intersectionality — Plain definitions with how each shows up in everyday institutions.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.
