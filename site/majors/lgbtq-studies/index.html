How can LGBTQ+ Studies students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for LGBTQ+ Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as define cisnormativity, heteronormativity, and intersectionality and summarize a foundational reading through to project-scale work like model intersectional survey outcomes. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a LGBTQ+ Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For LGBTQ+ Studies that means define cisnormativity, heteronormativity, and intersectionality, summarize a foundational reading and brainstorm paper topics on queer representation in media. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes synthesize sources across disciplines and analyze survey data on identity.

What do I need to start using AI as a LGBTQ+ Studies student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 LGBTQ+ Studies use cases here draw on 15 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for LGBTQ+ Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for LGBTQ+ Studies coursework at HWS?

Your LGBTQ+ Studies professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Model intersectional survey outcomes" exists to get you through LGBTQ+ Studies material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own.
