How can Spanish and Hispanic Studies students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Spanish and Hispanic Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as conjugate verbs with explanations and translate a short passage through to project-scale work like produce and verify a literary translation. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Spanish and Hispanic Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Spanish and Hispanic Studies that means conjugate verbs with explanations, translate a short passage and explain a grammar point. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes analyze a neruda poem and compare regional dialects.

What do I need to start using AI as a Spanish and Hispanic Studies student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Spanish and Hispanic Studies use cases here draw on 13 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Spanish and Hispanic Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Spanish and Hispanic Studies coursework at HWS?

Your Spanish and Hispanic Studies professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Produce and verify a literary translation" exists to get you through Spanish and Hispanic Studies material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own.
