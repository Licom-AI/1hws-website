Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. The 20 Greek and Roman Studies use cases here draw on 15 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Greek and Roman Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.
