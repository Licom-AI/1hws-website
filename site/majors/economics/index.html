Economics AI questions, answered

How can Economics students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Economics students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain an economic concept and summarize an nber working paper through to project-scale work like forecast a time series. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Economics major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Economics that means explain an economic concept, summarize an nber working paper and define economic terms. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes analyze a dataset in excel and compare economic theories.

What do I need to start using AI as a Economics student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Economics use cases here draw on 15 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Economics coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Economics coursework at HWS?

Your Economics professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Forecast a time series" exists to get you through Economics material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
