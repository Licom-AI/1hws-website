AI Use Cases for Environmental Studies at HWS

Learn more about the Environmental Studies program ↗

20 practical, difficulty-rated ways Environmental Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges can use AI — each naming the exact tutorial it links to and what you’ll take away. Click any card to watch it.

Before you use these on graded work: check your professor’s policy on AI. It differs by course, and these examples are study aids — not permission.

All Environmental Studies AI use cases

Easy

Define policy terms

Explain cap-and-trade, externality, or the precautionary principle clearly. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Environmental Studies student at HWS. My task: Define policy terms — Explain cap-and-trade, externality, or the precautionary principle clearly.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.

Easy

Draft a stakeholder email

Write an outreach message to a community organization for a project. The linked tutorial covers drafting an email in ChatGPT and improving it through conversation — the first draft is deliberately generic, then detail is added turn by turn — plus what not to paste in for privacy.

What you'll take awayWhy the first draft always reads like AI, how to iterate it into your own voice, and to keep real names out of the prompt.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Environmental Studies student at HWS. My task: Draft a stakeholder email — Write an outreach message to a community organization for a project.

Keep it under 150 words: polite, specific, with one clear ask. Give me two versions — one formal, one warmer. Don't include real names, ID numbers, or anything private.
