Define policy terms

Explain cap-and-trade, externality, or the precautionary principle clearly. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer, and the starter prompt below already frames it for Environmental Studies.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you — enough to use on Environmental Studies work this week.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm an Environmental Studies student at HWS. My task: Define policy terms — Explain cap-and-trade, externality, or the precautionary principle clearly.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.

Environmental Studies AI questions, answered

How can Environmental Studies students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Environmental Studies students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as define policy terms and summarize an environmental reading through to project-scale work like model a cost-benefit scenario. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Environmental Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Environmental Studies that means define policy terms, summarize an environmental reading and explain an environmental law. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes compare policy approaches and synthesize environmental justice sources.

What do I need to start using AI as a Environmental Studies student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Environmental Studies use cases here draw on 13 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Environmental Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Environmental Studies coursework at HWS?

Your Environmental Studies professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Model a cost-benefit scenario" exists to get you through Environmental Studies material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
