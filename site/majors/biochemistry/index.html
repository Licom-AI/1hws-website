How can Biochemistry students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Biochemistry students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as explain enzyme kinetics concepts and summarize a journal article's abstract through to project-scale work like perform statistical analysis of gene expression. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Biochemistry major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Biochemistry that means explain enzyme kinetics concepts, summarize a journal article's abstract and define metabolic pathway terms. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes build a study guide for a metabolism exam and compare protein purification methods.

What do I need to start using AI as a Biochemistry student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Biochemistry use cases here draw on 13 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Biochemistry coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Biochemistry coursework at HWS?

Your Biochemistry professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Perform statistical analysis of gene expression" exists to get you through Biochemistry material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
