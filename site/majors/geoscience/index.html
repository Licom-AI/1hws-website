Easy

Define geological terms

Explain unconformity, isostasy, or plate boundary types with examples. The linked tutorial covers ChatGPT's Study Mode (type /study), an interactive tutor that guides you with questions step by step instead of just handing over the answer, and the starter prompt below already frames it for Geoscience.

What you'll take awayHow to switch ChatGPT into tutor mode so it drills you on terms and ideas rather than doing the thinking for you — enough to use on Geoscience work this week.

Starter prompt — paste into ChatGPT
I'm a Geoscience student at HWS. My task: Define geological terms — Explain unconformity, isostasy, or plate boundary types with examples.

Define each term in one clear sentence, then give a concrete example of each in context. Once I've read them, quiz me on them one at a time and tell me what I got wrong and why.

Keep the terminology, examples, and level of detail appropriate for an undergraduate Geoscience course.

Geoscience AI questions, answered

How can Geoscience students use AI at HWS?

This page lists 20 practical AI use cases written specifically for Geoscience students at Hobart and William Smith Colleges — 10 easy, 6 medium, 4 hard by difficulty. They range from everyday coursework help such as define geological terms and summarize a research paper on sediment transport through to project-scale work like run gis terrain analysis code. Each one comes with a starter prompt you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Which AI use cases should a Geoscience major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Geoscience that means define geological terms, summarize a research paper on sediment transport and explain the rock cycle. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes interpret a geologic map and analyze geochemical data.

What do I need to start using AI as a Geoscience student?

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Geoscience use cases here draw on 13 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Geoscience coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Geoscience coursework at HWS?

Your Geoscience professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work. Everything on this page is a study aid: a use case like "Run GIS terrain analysis code" exists to get you through Geoscience material faster, not to produce something you submit as your own. If the syllabus is silent, ask before you use it.
