Which AI use cases should a Africana Studies major try first?

Start with the 10 rated Easy — they need no setup and work on the first try. For Africana Studies that means summarize a dense reading on pan-africanism, explain the concept of double consciousness and brainstorm essay topics on the harlem renaissance. Once those feel routine, move up to the Medium tier, which includes compare booker t. washington and du bois strategies and synthesize three articles on afrofuturism.

A free account with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is the whole list — no coding, no paid subscription, and nothing to install. The 20 Africana Studies use cases here draw on 16 tutorial videos between them, and each card ships with a starter prompt already framed for Africana Studies coursework, so the first thing you do is paste, not write.

Is it OK to use AI for Africana Studies coursework at HWS?

Your Africana Studies professor decides, and the answer changes from course to course and even between assignments — so read the syllabus before any of this touches graded work.
