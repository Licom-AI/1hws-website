## The narrative

Co-founded the HWS AI Club and Licom AI while playing Division III hockey well enough to win
a national championship and make the SUNYAC Academic Honor Roll. Now brings AI to a Florida
real estate developer — the intersection of, as he puts it, "two of my passions: artificial
intelligence and real estate development."

He is the club's public advocate. He wrote the article, did the podcast, and moved the
community onto Skool. His argument is sharper and more specific than a generic
AI-is-important pitch:

> Students aren't waiting for permission to use AI, they're just using it without guidance.

That is a genuinely good reason for a student AI club to exist, and it is *his* framing —
distinct from Hanna's "the gap isn't knowledge, it's implementation." The two complement each
other nicely and the site should use both rather than blurring them.
