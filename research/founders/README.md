## The short version

Zackary Hanna and Dominic Schimizzi co-founded the **Hobart and William Smith AI Club**
in **August 2025**. Two months later, in **October 2025**, they co-founded **Licom AI**,
a B2B AI consulting and implementation agency — Hanna as Founder/CEO, Schimizzi as
Founder/CSO.

Both are Economics students at HWS. Both have since moved into AI roles in industry:
Hanna at Enlaye (Harvard Innovation Labs), Schimizzi at Metro Development Group (Tampa).

Connor Shibley — listed as club President in the current site roster — joined Licom AI
as an AI Consultant, so the club and the company overlap in personnel.

## How to read these files

Each subject folder contains:

- `README.md` — the distilled profile, and the bio copy proposed for the website
- `linkedin-profile.md` — profile content as supplied
- `web-research.md` — independently searched/fetched findings
- `sources.md` — every URL, with what it did and didn't confirm

## Provenance and confidence

Facts are tagged throughout:

- **[verified]** — confirmed against a public page that was actually fetched
- **[supplied]** — provided directly by the user from the LinkedIn profiles
- **[unconfirmed]** — appears in one source only, or sources disagree

LinkedIn itself is **not machine-readable** — `linkedin.com` returns HTTP 999 to
WebFetch and Firecrawl refuses the domain outright. All LinkedIn content here is
**[supplied]**, pasted by the user from their own profile and their co-founder's.
It has not been independently re-verified and should be treated as self-reported.

Known source conflicts are recorded in each subject's `web-research.md` under
"Discrepancies" rather than silently smoothed over. Resolve them with the founders
before anything goes on a public page.
