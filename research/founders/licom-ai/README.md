## Handle this carefully on the club website

Licom AI is a **commercial venture**. The HWS AI Club is a **student organization at a college**.
Those are different things and the site should not blur them.

Recommendations:

1. **Mention it, don't market it.** Naming Licom AI in a founder bio is legitimate biography and
   genuinely establishes credibility. Listing its services, clients, or revenue on a college club
   site turns the club's page into lead generation for a private company.
2. **Skip the revenue figure.** The $60K number is self-reported and unverified, and a dollar
   figure on a student club's site reads as promotion regardless of accuracy.
3. **Skip the client list.** Valor Tax Relief, Blue Wave, Delex, CST — all fine on licom.ai,
   none belong on a club page.
4. **Be careful about linking.** A bio link to licom.ai is defensible. A call-to-action is not.
5. **Consider whether HWS has a policy** on student organizations promoting members' businesses.
   Many colleges do. Worth checking before this goes live.

The founder bios drafted in each person's `README.md` follow these rules: they name the company
and the role, and stop there.
