## Correction from the subject (2026-08-02)

Supplied directly by Zack after the research above was compiled. **Supersedes the sources.**

- He **now studies at Northeastern University**, not HWS.
- He sits on the **board of [Sundai](https://www.sundai.club/)** — the MIT/Harvard-affiliated
  Boston community that builds and launches AI prototypes every Sunday. Board membership is
  not listed on sundai.club, which names no leadership; this is on his word.

**This resolves discrepancy #1 below.** The LinkedIn education range of Aug 2025 – May 2026
was never a graduation date — it was a single year at HWS before transferring. "Sophomore"
and "junior" were both snapshots of a moving target.

His major at Northeastern is **not known** — he did not state one, and none is asserted on
the site. The published page says only "studies at Northeastern University," and moves the
Economics/Trustee Scholarship/Investment Club detail into the past tense as HWS history.

### 1. Class year: sophomore vs. junior — **RESOLVED, see above**

- zackhanna.com (fetched today): **"Junior"**
- Search-engine summary of the same site: **"sophomore"** — almost certainly a stale cached
  crawl from an earlier version of the page
- LinkedIn education entry: **Aug 2025 – May 2026**

**Assessment:** "Junior" is the current self-description and the freshest read. The
LinkedIn date range (Aug 2025 – May 2026) reads more like a single academic year than a
full degree span, so it may be an incomplete entry rather than a graduation date. **Ask him
for his actual class year rather than inferring one.**

### 2. Major: Economics vs. three majors

- LinkedIn *education* field: **Economics**
- LinkedIn *About* text: "studying **Economics, Computer Science, and Physics**"

**Assessment:** Not necessarily contradictory — the education field often lists only the
primary major. But "Economics, Computer Science, and Physics" is what he says in prose.
Confirm which framing he wants on the site.

### 3. Age / founding age

LinkedIn says he founded Licom AI "at 19" (Oct 2025) and that he is **21** as of the Enlaye
entry (May 2026). Those are ~7 months apart, which cannot span 19 → 21.

**Assessment:** One of the two numbers is off, or "at 19" refers to when he began building
rather than the incorporation date. **Avoid publishing a specific age.** It adds nothing to
a founder bio and is the single most likely detail to be wrong.
