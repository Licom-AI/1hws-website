## The narrative

Founded Licom AI out of his dorm room and grew it into a real consulting practice with a
team of six — ERP builds, logistics optimization, AI voice agents — while simultaneously
co-founding the HWS AI Club. Spent summer 2026 at Enlaye, an AI-native construction risk
platform in the Harvard Innovation Labs ecosystem, as the only undergrad on an all-Harvard
team.

The thread connecting all of it is the thing he says most often, and the thesis of his
most-read post (103K impressions):

> The gap isn't knowledge, it's implementation.

That is also, usefully, the club's reason to exist — which makes it the natural spine for
his bio on the site.

## Club contributions specifically

- Co-founded the club, August 2025
- Built the AI curriculum and ran hands-on workshops
- Demoed **OpenClaw** — a personal agent wired into his Mac mini, driven over WhatsApp with
  browser and Google Suite access — at a club meeting
- Brought in guest speakers, including **Lee Jokl** (AI strategy lead at T. Rowe Price, then
  first AI strategy hire at Unanet)
