# HWS Search Demand Research

**Prepared:** August 26, 2026
**Research tool:** OpenSEO MCP
**Project:** HWS AI Club (`ea176d55-59ff-4588-a827-c0c8ed195781`)
**Market:** United States / English

## What this file answers

This is a research snapshot of the searches and pages that currently bring the most
estimated organic traffic to the main Hobart and William Smith Colleges domain, plus the
searches that matter for campus clubs and events. It is not a claim that the HWS AI Club
should target every high-volume keyword owned by `hws.edu`.

## Evidence and limitations

- **OpenSEO domain report:** `get_ranked_keywords` for `hws.edu`, domain scope, organic
  results, sorted by traffic estimate, returned 5,837 keyword rows. The table below is a
  50-row snapshot, with the highest estimated-traffic rows first.
- **OpenSEO SERP report:** live top-20 Google results were checked for HWS clubs,
  organizations, student activities, and event searches.
- **Traffic estimate is not first-party traffic.** The `traffic` column is OpenSEO's
  estimated traffic value (ETV) for a ranking keyword/page, not Google Analytics sessions
  or Search Console clicks. Use it for prioritization only.
- **Search volume is an estimated monthly U.S. value.** A null value means OpenSEO did
  not return a reliable metric; it does not mean zero searches.
- **GSC is not connected** to the OpenSEO project. Google Search Console remains the
  authority for actual impressions, clicks, queries, indexation, and selected canonical.
- Rankings and estimates change by location, device, date, personalization, and provider
  refresh. Treat this as a dated snapshot, not a permanent ranking report.

### How to interpret the traffic leaders

1. **The true institutional brand is very strong.** Variants of “Hobart and William
   Smith” rank first and have the largest clearly school-related estimated traffic.
2. **Alumni and guest-speaker pages generate substantial non-campus traffic.** Christopher
   McDonald, James Carville, Brad Falchuk, Bill Whitaker, and other public figures account
   for many high-volume rows. This is real domain traffic but is not a useful target for an
   HWS student club.
3. **Student-service navigation is another major demand class.** PeopleSoft, academic
   calendar, tuition, acceptance rate, campus map, housing, and similar queries are owned
   by the appropriate HWS service pages. The AI Club should not create competing pages for
   those topics.

## HWS clubs and events SERP findings

OpenSEO checked live Google results for the following query set:

- `HWS clubs`
- `HWS events`
- `HWS student organizations`
- `HWS student activities`
- `Hobart and William Smith clubs`
- `HWS clubs and organizations`
- `HWS event calendar`
- `Hobart and William Smith events`

The dominant pattern was consistent:

- `www.hws.edu/offices/student-engagement/clubs-and-organizations.aspx` ranked #1 for
  HWS clubs, student organizations, and the full-name clubs query.
- `www.hws.edu/offices/student-engagement/activities/default.aspx` ranked #1 for HWS
  student activities and appeared near the top for clubs and events.
- `www.hws.edu/news/calendar.aspx` ranked #1 for HWS events, HWS event calendar, and
  full-name HWS events.
- HWS Programs and Events, Student Life, and HWS Engage FAQ pages repeatedly appeared
  in the top results.
- `hws.campuslabs.com/engage/organizations` appeared for organization-directory intent.
- Instagram and Facebook appeared for social/event discovery.
- Some full-name event/club searches included an AI Overview.

Google Search Console is still not connected inside the OpenSEO project, so this is
third-party keyword evidence rather than the site's actual impressions or clicks. The
implemented page therefore targets `HWS events calendar` and `HWS events`, answers common
event/academic-calendar/club questions in visible text, and routes navigational academic
and club searches to the authoritative HWS pages. No volume was assigned to keyword
variants OpenSEO omitted.

### What this means for the HWS AI Club

The club should not try to outrank HWS's general Clubs page for every generic campus
search. The realistic opportunity is to own the specific answer inside that category:

- `HWS AI Club`
- `Hobart and William Smith AI Club`
- `HWS AI Club student organization`
- `HWS AI Club meetings`
- `HWS AI Club workshops`
- `HWS AI Club events`
- `how to join the HWS AI Club`
- `AI resources for HWS students`
- `AI for [major] students at HWS`

Those pages need accurate meeting/event facts, a clear joining action, and links from the
official HWS Engage or student-organization listing. Keywords alone will not overcome the
authority gap shown in these SERPs.

## Recommended page-to-intent map

| Search intent | Recommended destination | Content requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the club | Homepage | Full organization name, HWS affiliation, Geneva location, who can join, and Skool link |
| Decide whether to join | FAQ / join section | Cost, eligibility, experience level, meeting schedule, and direct join path |
| Find an upcoming club event | Event page or current homepage section | Real event name, date, time, location, access instructions, and an update/expiry process |
| Find HWS AI resources | `/resources/ai-at-hws/` | Cited Library/Career Services resources plus clear distinction between official policy and club guidance |
| Find AI help by major | `/majors/<major>/` | Major-specific examples, HWS program context, responsible-use guidance, and internal links |
| Find all HWS clubs | Official HWS Clubs page / CampusLabs | The club should request a website link from the official listing rather than duplicate the directory |
| Find the full HWS event calendar | Official HWS Community Events Calendar | Link to the official calendar when relevant; do not imply the club owns it |

## What “most traffic” should mean for this project

Use three separate measures:

1. **Domain demand:** OpenSEO's estimated traffic for `hws.edu` reveals broad search
   behavior and the pages that have authority. It is useful context, not the club's
   performance.
2. **Club demand:** OpenSEO SERP checks and a future rank tracker show whether the club
   appears for specific club/resource/event searches.
3. **Actual performance:** GSC impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed pages,
   and selected canonicals for `www.hwsaiclub.com`. This is not available until the GSC
   property is connected.
